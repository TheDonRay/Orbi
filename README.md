# 🌐 Orbi

> **Powered by Orbi — Stay in Orbit, Stay Ahead.**

Your personal digital secretary that keeps you organized and informed without the noise.

## 🎯 What is Orbi?

Orbi is a smart daily briefing system that delivers everything you need to start your day in one concise email:

- **📋 Your Daily Agenda** - Tasks and priorities broken down into a manageable action plan
- **📰 News Digest** - Latest headlines summarized so you can stay informed in minutes

Whether you're juggling a packed schedule or just want clarity to start your day, Orbi acts as your intelligent assistant — keeping you focused, productive, and in the know.

## 🛠️ Tech Stack

- **Backend**: Express.js
- **Frontend**: React *(potentially — still exploring options!)*

> **Note**: The frontend implementation is still under consideration. The project may remain backend-focused with email as the primary interface. Stay tuned for updates!

## 🚀 Features (Planned)

- Daily personalized email briefings
- Task prioritization and organization
- Curated news summaries
- Clean, distraction-free experience
